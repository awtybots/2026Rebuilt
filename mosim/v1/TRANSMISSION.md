# Goldfire V1 Fuel mechanism transmission

The stowed and extended Onshape exports give the shaft axes, pulley teeth, gear stages, and belt paths. The extended export seats the intake pulley and belt models on the roller axes. Positive CAD-forward in the Unity prefab is toward the intake. For spindexer directions, look down on the robot with the intake at the top of the page.

| Mechanism | Motor-to-roller path | Forward command |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Carriage NEO Vortex → 3:1 MAXPlanetary → two separate 70T GT2 belts in parallel, with inferred 27T drivers and labeled 26T pulleys on the two 2 in flex-wheel shafts. | Same rotation on both shafts. The leading faces move upward and the top surfaces move toward the hopper. Each shaft runs at 27/78 of motor RPM. |
| Hopper spindexers | One NEO Vortex per side → 3:1 MAXPlanetary → 28T/28T open 120T HTD belt → 4 in omni wheel. Java leads with CAN 15 and inverts follower 16. | The left wheel turns clockwise and the right wheel counterclockwise from above. Both inner faces move toward the shooter. Each shaft runs at one-third of motor RPM. |
| Kicker | CAN 13 and 14 → 3:1 MAXPlanetary on each side → 23T/23T open 110T HTD belts to the powered shaft. The powered shaft also drives the 3 in lower roller via 24T/24T open 60T HTD belts and the 2 in upper roller via 33T/33T open 70T GT2 belts. | All three shafts turn in the same direction on the shared horizontal axis and run at one-third of motor RPM. |
| Main flywheels | CAN 9/10 for one side and 11/12 for the other → 23T/23T open 120T HTD belt on each side → 4 in flywheels. | Both main shafts spin in the same physical direction and run at motor RPM. The 1,735 RPM manual command means 1,735 RPM at the flywheel shaft. |
| Backspin rollers | Each main flywheel shaft → 27T/27T open 110T HTD belt → intermediate shaft → one 34T/34T external spur mesh → upper 2 in roller → 39T/39T open 90T 3 mm GT2 belt → lower 2 in roller. | Both backspin shafts spin opposite their main flywheel at the same shaft RPM. Their smaller diameter gives half the main flywheel surface speed. |

The two intake belt spans measure 65.2 mm and 65.3 mm center to center in the extended assembly. The motor-side belt arcs are about 0.45 mm larger in radius than the roller-side arcs. A 27T/26T pair on an open 70T belt at 3 mm pitch predicts a 65.25 mm center distance and a 0.48 mm pitch-radius difference. The motor-side 27T count is inferred from those measurements. The two fixed 4:1 MAXPlanetary slices are outside the moving intake wheel path. The Java intake CAN declarations are 18 and 19 and are marked unknown in that revision.

The mod uses MoSim `RollerGroup` multipliers for shaft ratio and the flipped setting for direction. An `InteractionRoller` at each shaft applies contact forces to Fuel and drives the corresponding mesh. The 3:1 groups use 6,784 motor RPM as the nominal 12 V ceiling, which gives 2,348 RPM at an intake shaft and 2,261 RPM at a spindexer or kicker shaft. The real Java commands of 10,000 RPM for intake and kicker and 9,000 RPM for the spindexers are above the motor's published free speed. Real shaft RPM under load is lower. Fuel contact is modeled at the shafts and through tuned contact forces along the intake, kicker, and shooter outlet. The shooter outlet uses the local flywheel and backspin surface speeds. The 16 lightweight belt meshes wrap the measured pulley radii and meet both rims tangentially. The four upper shooter belts are 9 mm wide. The flywheel-to-backspin spans shift sideways by 3.175 mm to sit centered in both pulley grooves. Both intake belts travel with the carriage and run over a simplified two-groove driver built from the measured motor axis and inferred 27T size. The transmission pulleys and gears follow the signed RPM of their corresponding shafts. The four swerve treads roll on separate pivots based on the chassis velocity along each steered wheel direction.
